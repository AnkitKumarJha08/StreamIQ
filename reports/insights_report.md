# 📊 StreamIQ – Insights Report

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze Netflix dataset to uncover content trends and patterns.

## 📌 Key Findings

- Movies dominate the platform (~70%)
- Drama and Comedy are the most common genres
- Content growth increased rapidly after 2015
- USA is the leading content producer
- Most movies are between 80–120 minutes

## 💡 Business Insights

- Netflix focuses on mass-consumption genres
- Global expansion led to rapid content growth
- Content strategy favors quantity + diversity

## 🚀 Conclusion

The analysis shows that Netflix prioritizes scalable content production with a focus on popular genres and global reach.
